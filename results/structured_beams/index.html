Ho:YAG structured-beam calculator

Choose the solver, phase mask, and seeded random Ho distribution, then press Calculate. Each run evaluates six 1 W input shapes—Gaussian, two helical LG modes, HG(1,1), a Bessel-Gaussian needle, and a flattop super-Gaussian—and returns irradiance, phase, and centerline side profiles.

The modal estimate calculates one saturated fixed-mode oscillator background for all six shapes, then probes each 1 W beam separately. It recomputes heat, cooling, stress, and optical phase for the Ho map. The probes do not deplete the inversion or heat the crystal; the hot optics do not feed back into the oscillator.

Waiting for a calculation.

The phase mask is ideal and phase-only: irradiance is unchanged immediately across the SLM. The Ho map is regenerated from the seed on every calculation. The modal estimate is slower and subject to the local supervisor time budget.
