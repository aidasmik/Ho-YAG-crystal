12 at.% Yb:LuAG pulsed proposal setup

10 nJ, 10 kHz seed from a femtosecond laser, stretched before amplification. A 100 µm disk receives ten alternating pump passes; ten ideal signal traversals are an exploratory optical layout. The proposal gives the seed energy, rate, disk, pump diameter, and pump-pass count. The 300 fs source, 10 ps stretched duration, 40 W pump, beam waist, and 938/1030 nm centers are editable modeling assumptions.

Proposal targets and model scope

Target: >100 µJ output, gain >10,000, pattern fidelity >0.8, phase distortion <0.5 rad. Coating requirements: front AR reflection <0.1% at 1030 nm, rear HR >99.9% over 940–1090 nm, damage threshold >10 J/cm². These are specifications, not measured coating performance.
