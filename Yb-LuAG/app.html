Yb:LuAG pulsed disk

The source is always a Gaussian TEM00 seed. Choose the target field at the crystal; a phase-only SLM mask is applied to the Gaussian and the beam propagates to the disk. Optional phase corrections are added to that mask. The model follows the 10 kHz pulse train through one 100 µm disk and a generic copper cooler.
